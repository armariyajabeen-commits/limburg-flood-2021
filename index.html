Age profile, share of residents

Area AArea B

Five age groups from the CBS buurt statistics, weighted by the residents each circle counts in each buurt. This is a buurt-level proxy (an ecological estimate): the 100 m grid suppresses small counts, so a direct age split is only known for part of the people.

On the map, each buurt a circle touches is shaded in that circle's colour. The darker the shade, the larger the share of its 100 m cells whose centre lies inside the circle.

Exposure and profile

IndicatorAB
Observed. The blue extent is the maximum flooded area of July 2021 (4TU.ResearchData). It is a preliminary reconstruction, partly modelled and improved by local knowledge and photographs, not a survey. "Exposed" below means inside that extent.
Modelled. The hatched teal layers (and the water-depth classes) are dike-breach scenarios from the national hazard maps (T = 10 to 10,000 years). They are design scenarios, not a record of what happened in 2021: they are drawn hatched and listed separately, and none of the exposure figures use them.

Where the numbers come from

    Population: CBS Vierkantstatistieken 100 m, 2021 (CC BY 4.0, CBS attribution required). Buurten: CBS Wijken en Buurten 2021 (CC0) via PDOK. Buildings and dwellings: BAG, Kadaster via PDOK OGC API (CC0), see the note on the 2021 snapshot below. Observed extent: Slager, de Moel & de Jong, 4TU.ResearchData, doi:10.4121/16817389.v1 (CC BY 4.0). Scenario layers: ROR hazard maps from the LDO geoserver (licence unclear). Terrain: AHN4 DTM via PDOK WCS. Land use: CBS BBG 2017. Roads: NWB. Water: Top10NL. Soil: BRO Bodemkaart. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors © CARTO.

    The BAG is served only as today's snapshot. The 2021 situation was reconstructed approximately from status, document date and construction year, so some buildings may differ from 2021. Circle figures count 100 m cells whose centre is inside the circle. "Exposed residents (C)" allocates each cell's residents to its BAG dwellings and counts dwellings inside the observed extent, assuming equal occupancy per dwelling in a cell; "(A)" weights each cell by the part of it inside the extent.

    The map layers for the modelled scenarios, buildings, land use, soil, water and roads are simplified for display only (holes under 3,000 m² in the scenario polygons filled, geometries simplified). All figures are computed from the unsimplified project data. Details and limits: Evidence & method.
